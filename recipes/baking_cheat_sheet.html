Cheat Sheet for Baking

Vegetables

  Temperature Time (minutes) Notes
Zucchini 425F 15-20mins Cut into half-moons or sticks; flip halfway. Do not overcrowd or they will steam.
Asparagus 425F 10-15mins Toss with oil and salt; roast until tender and tips are crisp.
Broccoli 425F 20-25mins Cut into florets; toss with oil and roast until edges brown.
Squash 400F 25-35mins (cubes) or 40-50mins (halves) Brush with oil; season with salt, pepper, or maple/cinnamon for sweetness.
Sweet Potatoes 400F 25-35mins (cubes) or 40-50mins (halves) Roast until soft inside and lightly caramelized outside.

Meat

  Temperature Time (minutes) Notes
Chicken Breast 400F 20-25mins Internal temp: 165°F (74°C). Brush with oil or marinade to keep moist.
Chicken Thigh 425F 35-45mins Internal temp: 175°F (80°C) for tender results. Skin should be crispy.
Ribs 300F 2.5 to 3 hours (covered), then 15 to 20 min at 425°F to finish Wrap in foil, slow roast until tender, then uncover and caramelize with sauce at high heat.
